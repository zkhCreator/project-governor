# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community. Participation is open regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint when conducted in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, licenses, and attribution.
- Welcome questions and reasonable mistakes.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and community boundaries.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention
- Personal insults, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption
- Publishing private information or credentials without explicit permission
- Knowingly submitting malicious code, deceptive evidence, or unlicensed material
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, lock, or remove contributions and interactions that violate this policy. Repeated or severe violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact methods available on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/zkhCreator) or GitHub's built-in abuse-reporting tools. Do not disclose sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Reports will be handled with discretion, subject to legal and platform obligations.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, release channels, and public interactions where a person is representing this project.
